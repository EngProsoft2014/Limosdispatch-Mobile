Appearance
==========
Account > Appearance > App theme offers Light, Dark and Ocean. The choice is
saved on the device and restored at startup, including the sign-in screen.
ThemeService updates dynamic resources without recreating the workspace or
interrupting an active journey. The navy brand header retains the original logo.

App.xaml defines the shared 44-unit field/button height, 22-unit page titles,
compact spacing, theme surfaces and separate tab-bar background. Shell uses
the same selected color for the tab label and icon. Platform handler mappings
remove native underlines from entries, pickers, date/time inputs, editors and
search. PasswordField is a reusable two-way-bound masked input with an eye
button. Showing the password only changes its display on the current screen.

SVG action arrows render at 24 units, with separate assets for primary and
secondary buttons. Sign in retains its arrow-free label.

Validation: Android build/run and iOS simulator-target build completed with
zero warnings/errors. Visually checked Android rides, expenses, earnings and
account in Dark, and account/sign-in in Ocean. Dark survived app restart.
The eye displayed and re-masked a local test value; saved credentials were
restored afterwards without changing their stored value. All 24 core checks
passed. This does not constitute an iPhone runtime verification.
