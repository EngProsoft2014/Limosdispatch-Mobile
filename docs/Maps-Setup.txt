iOS: FleetMapView uses the native Google Maps SDK through the iOS-only
AdamE.Google.iOS.Maps 9.2.0.8 binding and GoogleMapsHandler. The supplied iOS
key is initialized in AppDelegate before creating a map. MapKit is not used
by the tracking map on iOS. The standard Android MAUI Google Maps handler
continues to serve Android.

Native iOS packaging/runtime validation needs a Mac, Xcode and Apple signing.
Windows managed compilation was checked against the Google binding. Its
native framework downloader requires 7zip on Windows; the local managed-only
check skipped that download target. No native iPhone execution is claimed.

The tenant had no active chauffeurs at that time, so real live-driver markers
could not be visually validated during this UI pass.
